Stick orbit is a held modifier: hold Shift+MMB and deflect the stick to orbit the view — drag speed is tunable via stick_speed in ~/.config/g13/bindings-0.properties. Stick click sends a double middle-click, mapped in Fusion 360 to Fit view.
